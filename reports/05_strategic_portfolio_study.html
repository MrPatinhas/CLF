4. "Fechamento de Defesa" (The 5-Man Correlated Defensive Lock)

Locking 1 GOL + 2 LAT + 2 ZAG from the same club binds 5 roster slots to a single Bernoulli trial: if the Clean Sheet holds, you bank an instant +25.0 pt floor; if a single goal leaks in, all 5 players lose their SG simultaneously.

💊 Learnable Pill: Why Conditional Locking (P(SG) ≥ 38%) Beats Always-Locking

In rounds where no club exceeds P(SG) ≥ 38%, forcing a 5-man defensive lock is a negative-EV gamble that averages only 2601.3 pts/season. By switching to a conditional policy—activating the 5-man Fortress ONLY when a heavy favorite clears P(SG) ≥ 38% and defaulting to the 9S/2W Shield otherwise—the portfolio jumps to 2616.8 pts/season!
