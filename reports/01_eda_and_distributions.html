1. Positional Score Distributions & The Clean Sheet (`SG`) Bimodality

Why a single naive model struggles when pooling Goalkeepers, Defenders, Midfielders, Attackers, and Coaches together.

💊 Learnable Pill: Structural Bimodality in `GOL`, `LAT`, and `ZAG`
Look at the right-hand chart above: when a team keeps a Clean Sheet (SG = 1), Goalkeepers jump from an average of 2.28 pts to 8.44 pts, Fullbacks (LAT) jump from 2.48 to 7.82 pts, and Center-Backs (ZAG) jump from 1.58 to 6.89 pts! Meanwhile, Midfielders (MEI) and Attackers (ATA) receive zero SG bonus. This means ~65% of a defender's variance is driven by a team-level Poisson event ($P(\text{Opponent Goals} = 0) = e^{-\lambda_{\text{opp}}}$), whereas attackers are driven by individual shot & goal involvement.

2. Home Advantage & The Cross-Season Gap (Round 38 → Round 1)

Testing your hypothesis: how much predictive signal survives across the off-season break?

💊 Learnable Pill: Why We Need Dual-Memory Empirical Bayes Instead of a Hard Reset
Look at the right subplot above: in Rounds 1–5 of a new season, a player's previous season average still correlates with their current match score at $r \approx 0.17 - 0.25$! If we simply reset rolling windows to NaN or 0 in Round 1, our model forgets who Arrascaeta, Hulk, and Gómez are for the first month of the championship. Conversely, treating Round 1 of 2020 as Round 39 of 2019 overweights stale short-term form. Our Dual-Memory Empirical Bayes formula blends an off-season retention prior ($0.80 \times \mu_{S-1} + 0.20 \times \mu_{\text{pos}}$) with current-season appearances $n_S$, smoothly handing over control between Rounds 1 and 8.

3. Forensic Data Discovery: How 2020 Missing Round 37 Almost Poisoned Our Models

During our first model training run, Global LightGBM under-predicted 2024–2025 Midfielders. Investigating the training target revealed a subtle upstream trap in the raw `caRtola` repository.

💊 Learnable Pill: The Missing-Snapshot Cumulative Diff Trap
In henriquepgomide/caRtola, seasons 2018–2025 are stored as 38 cumulative CSV snapshots (rodada-1.csv ... rodada-38.csv). However, in 2020, rodada-37.csv was missing from the upstream repo, and in rodada-38.csv several hundred players had their entire season cumulative scouts dumped in a single step! Because raw points in that CSV was still single-round (mean 1.44), while differenced scouts summed to 53.38 points, any model trained on un-sanitized base_norm_score saw a massive phantom spike in 2020. Adding our automated sanitize_cumulative_bleed_anomalies() gate (comparing |base_norm_score - points| > 6.5) repaired all 1,115 corrupted rows (0.8% of data) and boosted Global LightGBM's 2024 lineup backtest from 1,794 pts to 2,661 pts (+867 pts!).
