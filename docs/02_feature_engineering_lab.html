1. The "Arrascaeta Signal": Rolling Windows vs. EWMA vs. Empirical Bayes Shrinkage

Visualizing how different temporal memory filters track an elite outlier across 60 matches without over-reacting to single-match noise or forgetting his talent in Round 1.

💊 Learnable Pill: Bias-Variance Trade-off in Window Length ($W=3$ vs EWMA vs Empirical Bayes)
Notice how the red dotted line (player_roll3_base_norm_score) whipsaws violently between 1.0 and 14.0 points every time Arrascaeta has a quiet match or a 2-goal explosion. Feeding only a 3-game average into a model introduces massive variance error. Meanwhile, the gold line (player_dual_eb_score) anchors around 6.0–7.5 points—consistently +3.0 points above the gray population midfielder line—while smoothly adjusting across seasons!

2. What Actually Predicts Each Position? (LightGBM Feature Gain)

Different positions live in different statistical universes. Inspect the top 10 features discovered by our specialized positional models.

💊 Learnable Pill: Market Efficiency (`pre_match_price`) + Tactical Physics (`expected_team_gf`)
  • Goalkeepers (GOL): Dominated by long-term keeper quality (player_dual_eb_score), shot-stopping propensity (player_ewma_DE), and regular starter status (rounds_since_last_played).
  • Defenders (LAT & ZAG): Heavily driven by team matchup strength (expected_team_gf, opp_eb_conceded_G, poisson_sg_prob) and floor tackling consistency (opp_eb_conceded_floor).
  • Midfielders & Attackers (MEI & ATA): Strongly guided by price_rel_to_pos, pre_match_price, player_roll10_base_norm_score, and opponent defensive porousness (opp_roll5_conceded_A, opp_eb_conceded_G).

3. Positional Opponent Vulnerability Matrix (2024–2025)

Not all "weak defenses" are weak in the same way: some clubs concede massive tackle/crossing points to Fullbacks (`LAT`), while others force opposing Goalkeepers (`GOL`) into high-save masterclasses.

💊 Learnable Pill: Why Level-2 Positional Conceded Features Matter
Look at the heatmap above: teams that shoot frequently from outside the box (like Palmeiras or Flamengo) actually give opposing Goalkeepers (`GOL`) higher save (`DE`) points even when winning, whereas clubs with weak wing defense bleed extra points specifically to opposing Fullbacks (LAT) and Attackers (ATA). Our opp_pos_conceded_roll5_* features capture this exact positional matchup asymmetry.
