Architectural Foundations

Discriminative vs. Generative Scoring

Standard Machine Learning treats Cartola as a regression problem. Sports physics treats it as an arrival process of discrete events.

Discriminative Regressor (GBDT / Neural Net)

Maps 104 contextual features directly to a single expected scalar score:

f(Home, Price, xG, Form, Opponent) → ŷ = 7.68 pts
  • Black-Box: Cannot explain how those points will be earned on the pitch.
  • Zero Uncertainty Shape: A single mean cannot distinguish between a steady 5-point tackle grinder and a volatile 5-point boom-or-bust striker.
  • Leaf Shrinkage: Regresses elite stars toward the position baseline.

Generative Rate Model (Poisson Count Process)

Models the underlying generative physics of football events:

Points = ∑ ws × Ns,   where Ns ∼ Poisson(λs)
  • Linearity of Expectation: 𝔼[Points] = ∑ ws × λs yields a 100% itemized grocery bill.
  • Full Probability Mass Function: Simulate 10,000 matches to compute the true 10th percentile floor, 90th percentile ceiling, and P(Mito ≥ 8).
  • Modular Tuning: Individual calibration for attacking bursts vs defensive duels.
Empirical Data Verification

The Real-World Dispersion Spectrum

We computed the empirical Mean and Variance across 131,660 active player appearances (2014–2025). Here is where pure Poisson holds—and where it breaks.

Scout Mean Variance Var / Mean Distribution Model
Goals (G) 0.078 0.084 1.07 Pure Poisson
Assists (A) 0.055 0.058 1.05 Pure Poisson
Shots on Target (FD) 0.208 0.239 1.15 Pure Poisson
Tackles (DS) 0.941 1.471 1.56 Quasi-Poisson
Fouls Suffered (FS) 0.813 1.216 1.49 Quasi-Poisson
GK Saves (DE) 0.207 0.880 4.26 Negative Binomial
Clean Sheet (SG) 0.110 0.098 0.89 Bernoulli B(1, p)

Key Statistical Takeaways

1. Pure Poisson Equidispersion (Var ≈ Mean): Outfield attacking actions (Goals, Assists, Shots on Target) follow an almost textbook Poisson arrival rate (λ ≈ 1.05–1.15).

2. The Goalkeeper Overdispersion Trap: Goalkeeper Saves (DE) exhibit a massive Var/Mean ratio of 4.26! In games where a team parks the bus, goalkeepers record 6 to 9 saves. Pure Poisson collapses on this tail. We use Negative Binomial (α = 0.32) to capture this heavy tail.

3. Clean Sheet (SG) is Binary: Clean Sheet is a team-level Bernoulli trial {0, 1}, not a count. It contributes +5.0 points with probability P(SG), sampled independently.

Mathematical Architecture

The Multiplicative Rate Formulation

How do we calculate the expected scout count λi,s for player i facing opponent j in venue v?

λi, s = μpos, s × θi, s × δj, pos, s × γs, home

1. Player Ability (θi,s)

Ratio of the player's Empirical Bayes shrunk historical rate relative to their positional baseline:

θi,s = EB_Rate(playeri, s) / Baseline(pos, s)

Pedro's goal index: 2.85× positional average.

2. Opponent Generosity (δj)

How much more of scout s this specific opponent concedes to this position:

δj = Opp_Conceded(teamj, pos, s) / League_Avg

Athletico-PR concedes 1.22× more shots on target away.

3. Venue Factor (γhome)

Venue physics: Home advantage varies significantly across different scout types:

γG, home = 1.18 • γDS, home = 0.92

Home teams shoot +18% more; away teams tackle +8% more.

Probabilistic Engine

The Monte Carlo Machinery

Once rates λs are known, we sample 10,000 matches in vectorized NumPy. A single scalar becomes a complete risk-reward probability distribution.

Pedro (High-Ceiling Striker)

λG = 0.45, λA = 0.18, High Goal Dependence

Floor (P10): 1.0 pts Mean: 7.01 pts Ceiling (P90): 15.6 pts

P(Mito ≥ 8.0 pts): 39% • P(SuperMito ≥ 12.0 pts): 21%
Massive right-skewed tail: 39% chance of explosive game, but 10% chance of ≤ 1.0 pt.

Tactical Playbook

Drafting by Floor vs. Drafting by Ceiling

Cartola fantasy strategy is not one-size-fits-all. Your optimal squad ranking changes based on your position in the season standings.

⇅ Mode A: Protecting 1st Place (High-Floor)

Objective: Minimize Portfolio Variance

  • Rank pool by Floor (P10) & Expected Mean.
  • Stack defenders with high Clean Sheet probabilities (P(SG) ≥ 50%).
  • Prioritize midfielders with guaranteed volume (Tackles DS ≥ 3.0, Fouls Suffered FS ≥ 2.5).
  • Avoid high-volatility wingers who depend solely on goals.
CLI Command: python run_inference.py

⇅ Mode B: Chasing the Leader (High-Ceiling)

Objective: Maximize Upper Tail Probability P(Y ≥ 12)

  • Rank pool by Ceiling (P90) & Mito Probability.
  • Pick attacking wingers and set-piece strikers (Samuel Lino, Pedro, Plata).
  • Target wingbacks with high final-third crosses vs. generous defenses.
  • Accept a lower floor (even negative points) to maximize 15+ point explosions.
CLI Output: Generative_Ceiling_Upside Squad
